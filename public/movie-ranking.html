The Best Movies According to Vijai

My top 3 movies of all-time.

Zindagi Na Milegi Dobara

A tale of three friends who stay united and are there for each other through good times and bad.

Border

The story of the Indian armed forces fighting bravely despite being heavily outnumbered, until the Air Force comes to their aid to defeat the enemy.

Uri: The Surgical Strike

Based on real events: the proud Indian armed forces strike back at the terrorists who attacked Indian soil and killed innocent civilians.
